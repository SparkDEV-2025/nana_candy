Our Heritage

A Sweet Story from Jama'are

Nestled in the heart of Jama'are Local Government, Bauchi State, Nana Maidala's Candy was born from a grandmother's kitchen and a community's love for authentic Northern Nigerian flavors. Every candy is handcrafted using time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

From the rich creamy goodness of Tuwon Madara to the satisfying crunch of Gullisuwa, each treat carries the warmth of our land and the hands that made it. We use only the finest local ingredients — freshly harvested groundnuts, pure tiger nuts, and the best peanuts from our community's farms.

Today, Nana Maidala's Candy serves families across the region — bringing joy at weddings, parties, and everyday moments. Taste the tradition. Share the love.
